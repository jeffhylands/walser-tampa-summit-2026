What's your move?

5 Sessions, One Full Day

You leave with questions worth asking and a clearer sense of where your plan has gaps. Some of the topics include:

Artificial intelligence

How AI is reshaping the global economy, disrupting industries, and creating new risks and opportunities. Learn why investment strategies may need to adapt as the AI revolution accelerates.

The "Fiscal House" philosophy

A framework where every dollar is assigned a specific job and every financial decision passes through the Tax Lens, bringing income, investments, taxes, healthcare, and estate planning under one coordinated strategy.

Tax mitigation

How proactive tax planning may help protect more of what you have built. Explore the impact of withdrawal strategies, Roth conversions, charitable planning, and other advanced strategies through real-world case studies.

Asset protection

How risk-transfer tools may help protect a portion of your retirement assets from market losses while creating reliable income designed to last throughout retirement.

Markets and AI

Capital Scarcity: How AI hyperscalers, global companies, and governments are competing for the massive amount of capital needed to fund the AI revolution, and what that could mean for interest rates, markets, and portfolio construction.

Legacy and healthcare

How healthcare costs, long-term care needs, estate planning, and beneficiary decisions can affect both your retirement and the legacy you leave. Learn why these areas must work together before a crisis occurs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you may want to know before reserving your seat.

Who is Tampa Retirement Live for?

Tampa Retirement Live is designed for investors, pre-retirees and retirees who want to better understand the financial decisions that can shape retirement income, taxes, investments, healthcare and legacy planning. It is not for anyone looking to get rich quick.

Will I be sold something?

No. The day is education. If you want to talk further, the team will be available afterward, but nothing is pitched from the stage and no one will chase you.

I already have a financial advisor. Why would I come?

The day is built to help you ask better questions. You can expect to leave with specific things to raise with the advisor you already have. A second opinion costs you one Saturday.

I'm already retired. Is this still relevant?

Yes. Planning doesn't stop the day you retire. Distribution order, tax brackets, required minimum distributions, and estate documents all keep moving. Much of the day is aimed squarely at people already drawing income.

Should my spouse or partner attend with me?

If retirement decisions affect both of you, we strongly encourage attending together. Couple tickets are $67, only $20 more than a single ticket, and Couple VIP is $147. Every attendee needs their own registration.

What is included with my ticket?

Every ticket includes access to the full Tampa Retirement Live program from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, lunch and complimentary parking at the Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel.

What does the VIP ticket include?

VIP guests receive priority seating, access to the post-event VIP experience from approximately 4:15 to 5:00 PM, a photo opportunity with Rebecca Walser and Charles Payne, and a copy of each speaker's book. Only a limited number of VIP seats are released.

Will I get to meet Charles Payne?

Every attendee sees Charles speak from the main stage, and he joins Rebecca and Tom Siomades for the late-morning panel. VIP ticket holders also join the VIP experience straight after the closing session, where they get a photo with Charles and Rebecca and a copy of each of their books. That is VIP only, which is why the limited number of VIP seats go first.
