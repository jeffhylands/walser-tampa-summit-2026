The agenda

Six topics, one morning

Every session is education, not a sales pitch. You leave with questions worth asking and a clearer sense of where your plan has gaps.

Retirement income

How savings becomes a paycheck that lasts. What your number actually is, which accounts to draw from first, and how to make work optional instead of obligatory.

Tax mitigation

The quiet drag most plans never account for. How distributions, conversions, and timing change what you keep — and what a tax attorney looks at that a statement won't show you.

Investment strategy

Positioning for growth without betting the retirement you already earned. Where risk belongs at 60 that didn't belong at 40.

Asset protection

One lawsuit or health event can undo decades of building. What proper titling, entity structure, and coverage actually shield — and what they don't.

Social Security timing

Claiming early, at full retirement age, or at 70 is not a personality question. How the math, your spouse, and your tax bracket decide it for you.

Estate & legacy

Wills, trusts, and beneficiary designations that transfer what you built without probate, delay, or a tax bill your heirs didn't see coming.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is the 2026 Tampa Bay Retirement Summit?

Saturday, October 17, 2026 at The Tampa Club in downtown Tampa. Registration opens at 8:30 AM, the program runs 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and VIP lunch follows immediately after.

Who should attend?

Individuals and couples above age 50 who are serious about getting to a point where work becomes an option rather than an obligation — while minimizing taxes and protecting what they leave behind. This event is not for anyone looking to get rich quick.

I already have a financial advisor. Why would I come?

The summit is built to help you ask better questions. Most attendees leave with two or three specific things to raise with the advisor they already have. A second opinion costs you a Saturday morning.

I'm already retired. Is this still relevant?

Yes. Planning doesn't stop the day you retire — distribution order, tax brackets, required minimum distributions, and estate documents all keep moving. Much of the morning is aimed squarely at people already drawing income.

Will I be sold something?

No. The morning is education. If you want to talk further, the team will be available afterward — but nothing is pitched from the stage and no one will chase you.

Can I bring my spouse or a guest?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. The Couple ticket covers two seats together at a lower price than two singles. Every attendee needs their own registration.

What's the difference between Single, Couple, and VIP?

Single is one seat. Couple is two seats together at a discount. VIP is two seats in the reserved front row, plus a private lunch with Rebecca Walser and Charles Payne, a photo with Charles, and a signed copy of Rebecca's book. Only 40 VIP seats are released.

Will I get to meet Charles Payne?

VIP ticket holders do. VIP includes a private lunch with Charles and Rebecca after the program, plus a photo. General admission attendees will see him speak from the main stage and take part in the live Q&A, but the lunch and photo are VIP only — which is the main reason those 40 seats go first.

Can I upgrade to VIP after I've bought a ticket?

Yes, while VIP seats remain. Call the events team at (866) 929-3258 and they'll handle the difference.

Is lunch included?

Coffee and a light breakfast are included with every ticket. Lunch is a VIP benefit — a private seated lunch with Rebecca Walser and Charles Payne after the program ends.

Where do I park?

Parking is complimentary. Full directions and parking instructions are emailed to every registered attendee the week of the event.

What's the dress code?

Business casual or smart casual. Come comfortable.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes, the venue is wheelchair accessible. Call ahead at (866) 929-3258 for any other accommodation and the team will arrange it.

Can I transfer my ticket if something comes up?

Yes. All sales are final and refunds aren't offered, but tickets are fully transferable. Call (866) 929-3258 and we'll change the name on your registration.

Is there a virtual option?

No. The summit is fully in person by design — the value is in the room, the questions, and the conversations afterward.

Are group rates available?

Yes, for groups of ten or more. Call (866) 929-3258 and ask for the events team.
