What's your move?

Six topics, one full day

Every session is education, not a sales pitch. You leave with questions worth asking and a clearer sense of where your plan has gaps.

Retirement income

How savings becomes a paycheck that lasts. What your number actually is, which accounts to draw from first, and how to make work optional instead of obligatory.

Tax mitigation

The quiet drag most plans never account for. How distributions, conversions, and timing change what you keep, and what a tax attorney looks at that a statement won't show you.

Investment strategy

Positioning for growth without betting the retirement you already earned. Where risk belongs at 60 that didn't belong at 40.

Asset protection

One lawsuit or health event can undo decades of building. What proper titling, entity structure, and coverage actually shield, and what they don't.

Social Security timing

Claiming early, at full retirement age, or at 70 is not a personality question. How the math, your spouse, and your tax bracket decide it for you.

Estate & legacy

Wills, trusts, and beneficiary designations that transfer what you built without probate, delay, or a tax bill your heirs didn't see coming.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is Tampa Retirement Live 2026?

Saturday, October 17, 2026 at the Sheraton Tampa Brandon Hotel in downtown Tampa. Registration opens at 8:00 AM, the program runs 9:00 AM to 3:45 PM, and lunch is 12:30 to 1:30 PM.

Who should attend?

Individuals and couples above age 50 who are serious about getting to a point where work becomes an option rather than an obligation, while minimizing taxes and protecting what they leave behind. This event is not for anyone looking to get rich quick.

I already have a financial advisor. Why would I come?

The event is built to help you ask better questions. Most attendees leave with two or three specific things to raise with the advisor they already have. A second opinion costs you one Saturday.

I'm already retired. Is this still relevant?

Yes. Planning doesn't stop the day you retire. Distribution order, tax brackets, required minimum distributions, and estate documents all keep moving. Much of the day is aimed squarely at people already drawing income.

Will I be sold something?

No. The day is education. If you want to talk further, the team will be available afterward, but nothing is pitched from the stage and no one will chase you.

Can I bring my spouse or a guest?

Yes, and we’d encourage it. The Couple tickets cover two seats together for less than two singles: $67 instead of $94 for general admission, or $147 instead of $194 for VIP. Every attendee needs their own registration.

What's the difference between the four ticket types?

There are four. Single ($47) is one general-admission seat. Couple ($67) is two seats together. Both include the full day and lunch. Single VIP ($97) adds the VIP experience straight after the closing session: a photo with Rebecca Walser and Charles Payne, a copy of each of their books, and both authors on hand to sign them. Couple VIP ($147) is all of that for two. Only 40 VIP places are released in total.

Will I get to meet Charles Payne?

Every attendee sees Charles speak from the main stage and takes part in the panel discussion. VIP ticket holders also join the VIP experience straight after the closing session, where they get a photo with Charles and Rebecca and have both authors sign their books. That is VIP only, which is why those 40 places go first.
